The work

Every piece, one of a kind

Everything here is a single piece — not one of a batch, not something that can be repeated. The glaze on each one belongs to one firing only. This is a record of what has come out of the kiln, not a catalogue.

    Lettering and logos

    Some of what I make carries words: a logo, a name, a date, a line of someone's own, fired onto the clay so it stays there for good. If that's something you'd like to talk about, the door is open.
