Every piece, one of a kind

Everything here is a single piece — not one of a batch, not something that can be reordered. The glaze on each one belongs to one firing only. When a piece finds a home it disappears from this page and does not come back.

    Your logo, your words, on clay

    I also take custom work. A logo for a café or a shop, a name, a date, a few words of someone's own — fired onto a piece so it stays there for good. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll tell you honestly whether I can make it.
